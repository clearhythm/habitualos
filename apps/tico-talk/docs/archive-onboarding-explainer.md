## Original content

Tico sets the scene, then plays each guest's line as it happens — you
don't need to tap anything to hear them.

When it's your turn, the mic button at the bottom lights up. Tap it and
respond the way you actually would on shift — there's no script, so just
roll with whatever you're handed.

Tico coaches you along the way and tracks your progress on each
sub-skill.
